## 1. Tenancy

- Strict data isolation per tenant (logical + physical partitioning where required).
- Per-tenant encryption keys (key service).
- Tenant-scoped APIs, queries, storage, and audit.
- Cross-tenant access denied and tested.

---

## 2. Policy-signing service

- Dual control (two approvers) before signing.
- Versioning and staged rings (canary 1%, ring 1 10%, full fleet) per P0.4 §6.
- Automatic halt on false-block spikes (TARGET: 2x baseline within 1 h).
- Rollback to previous signed version.

---

## 3. Token issuance and fleet budget

- Distributed fleet-budget accounting with leased budgets for partitions. The control plane issues and reconciles allocations. The node enforces scope, mode and spend under [P2.2 §2](P2.2_Enforcement_Controller_v1.md#2-degraded-operation-and-lease-ownership).
- Per-tenant kill switch halts issuance immediately and prevents execution on receipt by the node. Already issued disconnected leases are bounded by expiry and revocation freshness.
- Total outstanding tokens across regions never exceeds fleet cap (TARGET 2%).
- Leases expire as specified; no cap breach after heal.

---

## 4. Telemetry ingestion, anchoring, audit, retention

- Ingestion with schema validation; buffers at node on failure.
- Ledger anchoring (checkpoint hashes) for tamper evidence.
- Audit store (append-only, 365-day retention TARGET).
- Retention enforcement per field classification.

---

## 5. Storage, queueing, deployment, availability

- Storage/queueing/deployment patterns chosen by ADR (recorded).
- Availability, RPO, RTO as TARGETs (HYPOTHESIS): RPO <= 15 min, RTO <= 1 h (TARGET).
- Design for failure of any single region or component (multi-region, failover).

---

## 6. Load testing

- Load test at 10x target fleet size.
- Metrics: latency p50/p95/p99, error rate, throughput.

---

## 7. Access model

- No standing human access to customer data.
- Just-in-time elevation with approval and audit.
- Every operator action audited.
