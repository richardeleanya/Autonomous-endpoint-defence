## 1. Certificate hierarchy

- **Root CA:** Offline, air-gapped, multi-party quorum (P0.3 §2).
- **Intermediate CA:** Online HSM, issues device certs.
- **Device certificates:** Short-lived (TARGET 24 h, HYPOTHESIS), bound to tenant + device + trust level.
- **No custom cryptography; standard X.509.**

---

## 2. Enrolment flow

- Admin creates single-use, tenant-scoped, time-limited enrolment token (P0.3 §7.3).
- Device submits token + attestation (TPM quote / SE) + public key.
- Admin approval required before certificate issuance.
- Re-enrolment revokes prior cert; decommissioning revokes and removes device.
- Every enrolment event ledgered.

---

## 3. Attestation verification

- **TPM 2.0:** Windows and Linux; verify TPM quote bound to public key and device_id.
- **Secure Enclave:** Apple silicon only.
- **Software-only keys:** Lower trust level (L2/L1 per P0.3 §3) recorded in certificate extension.

---

## 4. Revocation under partition

- Short certificate lifetimes + cached revocation lists with grace window (TARGET 24 h, HYPOTHESIS).
- Under partition: use cached status; if grace expired, fail-closed for T2+; T0 continues.
- Emergency revocation via out-of-band channel takes precedence.

---

## 5. Tenant isolation and abuse detection

- Every lookup scoped by tenant_id; cross-tenant access denied.
- Rate limiting and abuse detection on enrolment and lookup endpoints.
- Tenant isolation tested (TST-P3-ID negative vectors).
