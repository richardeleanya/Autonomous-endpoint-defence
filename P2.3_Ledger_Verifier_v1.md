## 1. Node ledger design

- **Hash chain:** `entry_hash = SHA-256(prev_hash || canonical(entry))`; genesis hash fixed.
- **Per-entry signature:** Device key signs entry_hash; signature stored.
- **Merkle checkpoints:** Every N entries (TARGET 1024) or T (TARGET 5 min), compute Merkle root over entry hashes; sign checkpoint.
- **Crash-safe append:** Write-ahead log; fsync on commit; atomic sequence increments.
- **Retention:** Per field classification; expired entries purged with tombstone (hash retained for chain integrity).

---

## 2. Export and anchoring

- **Export bundle:** `entries.jsonl`, `checkpoints.jsonl`, `manifest.json` (P0.5 §4.4).
- **Server-side anchoring:** Control plane (evidence-anchor service) stores checkpoint hashes; deletion of local history detectable by comparing anchored checkpoints.
- **Privacy:** Sensitive fields hashed/redacted per classification before export (P0.5 §5).

---

## 3. Standalone verifier CLI

- **Command:** `meshledger verify --export <bundle> --anchors <anchors.json>` (required example).
- **Output:** entries count, chain status, signature status, checkpoint status.
- **Independently buildable:** Separate build target; no product dependencies; documented build instructions.
- **External verification:** Auditor recomputes hashes and Merkle roots; no trust in service.

---

## 4. Rotation, retention, privacy

- **Key rotation:** Device key rotation recorded as ledger event; old entries verifiable with old key.
- **Retention:** Per classification; purge with tombstone preserving chain.
- **Privacy:** Hash/redact sensitive fields (command lines) per P0.5 classification.
